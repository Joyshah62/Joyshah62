## <img src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/74038190/216122041-518ac897-8d92-4c6b-9b3f-ca01dcaf38ee.png" width="30" /> About Me

<img align="right" alt="Coding" width="400" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/74038190/229223263-cf2e4b07-2615-4f87-9c38-e37600f8381a.gif">

I'm a Computer Science grad student at **Rutgers University** (M.S., expected 2027) who builds AI systems that work in the real world. I've deployed ML models to **50+ enterprise clients**, published in **IEEE**, and currently teach **100+ undergrads** as a Part-Time Lecturer/TA for CS336.

🧠 **What I'm Into:**
- **LLMs & AI Agents** — autonomous systems that reason, plan, and use tools. Deep into LangChain, LangGraph, RAG pipelines, and local inference with open-source models.
- **NLP & Explainable AI** — not just training models but understanding *why* they make decisions. Transformer interpretability with Integrated Gradients and LIME.
- **Full-Stack AI Applications** — end-to-end product thinking. I own the whole stack from PyTorch to React to Docker to AWS.
- **Production ML** — I care about latency, reliability, and deployment. Models that only work in notebooks don't count.

🎯 **What I'm Looking For:**
- **Summer 2026 SWE / AI/ML internships** where I build things that ship to real users.
- Teams working on LLM applications, NLP systems, ML infrastructure, or AI-powered products.
- I bring experience across the full stack — from training BERT classifiers and building fraud detection systems to writing Flask/FastAPI APIs and deploying with Docker + CI/CD.
